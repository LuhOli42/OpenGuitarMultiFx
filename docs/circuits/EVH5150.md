# 5150-Style Amplifier

A component-level model of the **Peavey/EVH 5150** ULTRA (Lead) channel, built from the factory
Peavey EVH 5150 schematic (5 pages, obtained as PDF). The 5150 is one of the defining high-gain
amplifiers, known for its aggressive, tight distortion and mid-forward voicing that became the
standard for modern metal rhythm tones.

Display name: **5150-Style Amplifier** (trademark-safe convention).

## Architecture

**ULTRA channel only** is modelled. Clean/Crunch channel switching, effects loop, built-in
reverb, and channel relay circuitry are not implemented. Solid-state rectification only.

### Preamp (five cascaded 12AX7 gain stages + cathode follower)

The 5150 has MORE preamp gain stages than any other amp in this project (5 gain stages vs 4 for
the Dual Rectifier or Mark IIC+).

1. **V1A** (220K plate from V1/E rail, 68K grid stopper, 1M grid leak, 1.82K/1µF bypassed cathode)
   - Input stage. R22=68K grid stopper, R2=220K plate load, R12=1.82K cathode, C3=1µF bypass.

2. **22nF coupling** → **470K bias ref** → **ULTRA Gain pot** (1MA audio taper, split rGainTop/rGainBot)
   - The ULTRA channel's pre-gain control (VR2=1M).

3. **V1B** (100K plate from V1/E rail, 470K grid leak, 1.82K/1µF bypassed cathode)
   - Second gain stage. R3=100K plate, R82=470K leak, R17=1.82K cathode, C4=1µF bypass.

4. **V2A** (100K plate from V1/E rail, 470K grid leak, 39K series stopper, 1.82K/1µF bypassed cathode)
   - Third gain stage. R1=100K, R6=470K, R15=39K series, R13=1.82K, C5=1µF.

5. **V2B** (220K plate from V2/D rail, 1M grid leak, 10K unbypassed cathode — compression stage)
   - The unbypassed cathode creates local negative feedback, reducing gain and softening clipping.
     Similar role to the Dual Rectifier's V2B but with a lower cathode R (~10K vs 27K).

6. **V5B** (220K plate from V2/D rail, 1M grid leak, 100K series stopper, 2.2K/1µF bypassed cathode)
   - Fifth gain stage. R96=220K, R87=1M, R101=100K, R102=2.2K.

7. **V5A cathode follower** (via `addFollower`)
   - Buffer between the preamp and tone stack. DC offset measured in a two-pass build.
   - Plate connected to V2/D rail.

### Tone stack

Fender-derived TMB with 5150-specific values:
- 33K slope resistor (R24)
- 470pF treble coupling cap
- 250K treble pot (VR5), 1M bass pot (VR3), 50K mid pot (VR4)
- .022µF bass and mid caps
- 22K series resistor between treble and bass junctions
- Post (master volume, VR8=1M) as a rheostat after the tone stack

### Post-tonestack gain recovery (V3B)

A feature unique to the 5150 among the amps modelled so far: a 12AX7 gain stage AFTER the tone
stack and master volume, recovering the signal level before the phase inverter.

- 100K plate load from PI/V3 rail (sC)
- 1M grid leak
- 100K series stopper from the master volume coupling
- 1K cathode bypassed with 1µF
- Coupling to PI through 22nF cap

This stage is always built (both reducedOrder and full model), since it sits between the tone
stack and the PI.

### Phase inverter (V4A/V4B, 12AX7 long-tailed pair)

- V4A: 82K plate (R46), V4B: 100K plate (R58) — both from PI/V3 rail
- R48=100K feedback from V4B plate to V4A grid (LTP cross-coupling)
- 1K + 10K split tail (same pattern as Dual Rectifier)
- 1M grid leaks: R50 (V4A, to tail midpoint), R53 (V4B, to NFB feedback point)
- 75pF compensation cap between plates
- .047µF coupling caps to power tubes
- kg1 × 40 on PI triodes (loop stability)

### Power amplifier (4 × 6L6GC as two push-pull pairs)

- .047µF coupling caps from PI to power grids
- 2.2K grid stoppers (R206/R202), 220K grid leaks (R55/R54) to fixed bias
- -52V fixed bias supply
- Fitted 6L6GC pair model with kg1 × 40 (kg1 split between PI and power, per tube-model
  loop instability memory)

### Supply chain

Unique to the 5150: R210=400Ω resistive drop between the plate rail (sA, ~465V) and the screen
rail (sB, ~441V), instead of the choke used in other amps. This gives the screens a stiffer,
faster supply.

- Solid-state rectification, 60Ω effective resistance
- sA (220µF, 100K bleeder) → R210=400Ω → sB (220µF, screens) → 4.7K → sC (30µF, PI/V3)
  → 10K → sD (30µF, V2) → 22K → sE (30µF, V1)
- 5 current sources tracking plate, screen, PI/recovery, V2, and V1 draws

### Output transformer and speaker

- Push-pull transformer with 3 coupled inductors (primary halves + secondary)
- J1=8 ohm matched, J2=4 ohm available; 16 ohm via Speaker control
- NFB: R56=39K from OT secondary → PI grid 2 feedback point, scaled to 78K for 16 ohm model
- Presence: VR9=10K pot shunting the feedback path, with .01µF cap and 1.5nF stray

### Reduced-order mode

The `reducedOrder` path (default in the registry) builds only the tone stack + recovery stage
in the power block. The behavioral power stage reads from the recovery plate voltage and applies
a saturating curve + sag lookup + shelf filter, same pattern as all other amps.

## Implementation notes

- The power block has 35 MNA unknowns (due to tone stack + recovery triode + PI + power amp +
  speaker all in one block). This required raising `NodalCircuit::maxUnknowns` from 32 to 48.
  The Dual Rectifier's power block has 31 unknowns; the extra 4 come from the recovery stage
  nodes (gRec, kRec, coupRec, wRecoveryPlate).

- R48 (100K from V4B plate to V4A grid) is the PI's cross-coupling resistor that creates the
  phase-splitting action. This is a direct resistor in the LTP, not through a capacitor.

- The sag table is scaled from the Twin Reverb's measured data (497V → 465V factor).

- Unity trim: -23.2 dB (the 5 gain stages + recovery produce significantly more gain at noon
  than any other modelled amp).
